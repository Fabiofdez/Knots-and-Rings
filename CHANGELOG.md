### Additions
- New Feature: Better Saplings!
- Saplings now have growth stages, from a tiny sprout all the way to young trees (and an extra giant stage for 2x2 trees)
- Saplings can now be pruned (at any stage) by using shears to stop their growth, and can resume growing with bonemeal
- Sapling placement now adds random offsets (like grass, ferns, etc.)
- Later stages of saplings are 2 blocks tall, and 2x2 saplings rearrange to grow from a central stem with the other saplings around it decaying gradually (with no effect to the final grown tree)
- Saplings at the sprout (earliest) stage decay when stomped, and can be recovered with bonemeal, but stomping a decayed sprout may destroy it!
- All saplings/stages have texture variations
- Remodeled the hanging mangrove propagule to match its new saplings

### Changes
- Log block models slightly simplified (better performance)
- Log edges now require CTM again (Known issue: log/wood edges temporarily removed for environments using Fusion due to performance issues and visual artifacts)
- Base resource packs now enabled by default

### Fixes
- Resolve z-fighting and model gaps for logs
- (Regions Unexplored) Fix visual bugs with overlay edges for eucalyptus/pine/silver birch logs
