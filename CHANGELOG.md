### Additions
- New Feature: Tree Seeds!
- New items (acorns, seed pods, cones, etc.) that grow into trees
- Tree leaves now drop their respective seeds instead of saplings
- Seeds can be composted, and uproot when stomped

### Changes
- When first placed, saplings have their original "sapling" growth stage instead of the "sprout" stage
- The vanilla Mangrove Propagule is renamed to Mangrove Sapling
- Mangrove leaves now drop mangrove propagules (the new seed item)
- Sapling stems now correctly yield their saplings when using Pick Block

### Fixes
- Modified hanging mangrove propagule models to fix z-fighting
- (Stay true compat) Removed tint from young oak/dark oak saplings which caused particles to be darker than expected
